II.The search

A question the corpus cannot match word for word still finds the passages that mean it. This is the retrieval step in every RAG pipeline, with nothing hidden behind a hosted endpoint.

WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT

Type a question, one the corpus does not contain and phrased the way a person would phrase it, and the plate answers with passages from Poe that mean it. Nothing is matched on keywords: the query becomes a vector, the vector is compared with every passage's vector, and the nearest ones come back.

Two things are working together. The index is a vector store holding one row per passage, built before deployment and shipped as a file; the rerank is the server's own Lua script, which re-orders the index's candidates with the model itself. The first is approximate and fast; the second is exact and small. That pairing is what a production retrieval stack looks like.

WHAT JUST HAPPENED

Your query went to the sandbox once, through the allowlist the console uses, and came back as a vector: the same model, the same normalization and the same window that embedded the corpus, which is the only way the distances mean anything. The vector never left this machine again.

The nearest passages came from a vec0 table in a SQLite file your browser downloaded: an exact k-nearest-neighbour search, your CPU, no server. Their text and their work and year come from the same file. Then the seven candidates were handed to the sandbox's preloaded rank.lua, which embedded all eight texts and re-ordered the seven by cosine to the query.

The rerank is bounded by the sandbox, not by politeness: the bridge charges one unit of work per declared text and refuses a request over eight, so a rerank carries at most seven candidates. The plate sends exactly seven and says so.

WHY IT MATTERS

Retrieval is the step that turns a language model from something that has read the internet into something that can read your corpus. The pattern is always the same: embed the query with the model that embedded the documents, find the nearest documents, put them in the prompt. This plate is that step with the prompt replaced by the page.

It is also the honest version of the arithmetic. Every layer states its precision — int8 weights, int8 index vectors, a measured recall of — at ten — because a retrieval system that will not tell you its recall is asking you to trust its vibes.

THE EXACT COMMANDS

Three commands, in the order the plate ran them. The first is the query embedding; the second is the search, which runs in your browser against the shipped file; the third is the server-side rerank.
