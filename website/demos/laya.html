IV.The decision

An email arrives; a real bi-encoder finds the most similar resolved tickets, and one command answers the original implementation's own five questions. Two models, one server: retrieval and decision.

Two models, one inbox: retrieval finds the similar case, a single forward pass decides the new one.

THE DECISION · 2 models · 5 questions · real retrieval · 1 forward pass · 0 input tokens · 32-dim decision miniature

WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT

Two models, one inbox. A real bi-encoder — minilm — finds the past resolved tickets most similar to the new email (the gem's own shortlist idea, with a retriever the gem tells you to supply). Then the decision model answers the gem's Presets.email_questions verbatim, all five questions in one forward pass.

The honesty bar: the retrieval leg is real — those cosines are minilm's. The decision leg runs a 32-hidden miniature of the Laya architecture (random weights, real mechanism): its answers are shapes, and the production swap is one model path.

WHAT JUST HAPPENED

First the retrieval leg: the email, embedded once and compared with five past resolved tickets — EMB.EVSHA minilm <embed.lua sha1> 1 "<email>" "<past ticket>" per candidate, real cosines, drawn as bars with the closest case lit.

Then the decision leg: one EMB.EVSHA laya <laya.lua sha1> 1 "<email>" "<questions>" "<config>" — every question scored in one pass, so the five rows come back together. The axis under the sheet reads the confidences without words: the dots land at the floors (a choice's 0, a yes/no's 0.5) because this checkpoint decides nothing, and 1 is a decided answer.

WHY IT MATTERS

Retrieval finds the similar case; decisions route the new one — two models, one server, and each leg is one round trip from a Redis client you already have. The questions are data, so the preset lives in the repo and diffs in review.

The sheet's confidence has a floor — 0 (or 0.5 for a yes/no) means a coin flip, the genuinely ambiguous email — so a threshold on it routes ambiguity to a human, and every option was scored, not hallucinated.

THE EXACT COMMANDS

The two legs' exact commands — embed.lua for the similarities (one call per past ticket), laya.lua for the decision (one call for all five questions). Point the decision call at a published checkpoint and it is the production integration.
