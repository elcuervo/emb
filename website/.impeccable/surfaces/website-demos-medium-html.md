# Surface brief — the medium

## Scope and visitor mode

**Read and play.** One multimedia plate at `website/demos/medium.html` for
`emb`, the gallery's fourteenth and last. The visitor arrives from the gallery
or a search and the plate answers *can one space hold more than one medium* — a
photograph and a clip reduced to a contact sheet are both pictures, and a typed
phrase ranks them together — and then shows the edge: a sound reduced to a
spectrogram, which the same model reads as a near-tie. The plate extends the
gallery's committed world unchanged: palette, type ladder, five teaching
sections, dark-plate apparatus and caption discipline come from `DESIGN.md`, and
it spends one accent per figure.

## The visitor's job

Type a phrase — or leave the shipped one — and watch it become an embedding on
the sandbox, then watch the map's halos grow and a ranked ledger fill with
photographs and clips ordering themselves by the same cosine; play the nearest
clip. Below, read the clip's contact sheet and the sound's spectrogram with the
same eight labels, swap the frame count and the frequency axis, and watch the
clip stay right while the sound's winner stays wrong and its margin collapses.
Leave knowing that a medium is searchable once it is a picture the model reads,
and that a picture of a sound is not the sound.

## Constraints (inherited, non-negotiable)

- Inherit the world in `DESIGN.md`. No new colour, font, asset, dependency or
  component language; the plate composes the gallery's existing mark, edge,
  bar, rule and annotation atoms, and adds only plate-local layout.
- No upload. Every medium and every picture ships committed; the only visitor
  input is text.
- No invented number. The count, the kinds, the dimension and the model name are
  read from the shipped index; every ranking and margin comes from the model's
  own reply.
- The plate states what an image match does not prove, as load-bearing copy.
- Video and audio are both present and playable, and neither autoplays.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One space, drawn from the model's own vectors, in which a photograph and
a contact sheet are the same size of thing — and a seam below it where that
stops. The plate is a **field of marks over a comparison sheet**: the media
placed where they project with a halo that answers the phrase, then the clip and
the sound read side by side with the margin printed under each. It refuses the
category default of a search box over a list of thumbnails: the space and the
ranking are one figure, and the honest non-answer is shown, not hidden.

OWN-WORLD: The gallery's atlas vocabulary — hairline rules, tracked mono labels,
the dark plate, `--accent` on the single nearest mark and the top bar. The media
are the only chromatic material and are never framed, tinted or shadowed beyond
a hairline.

STORY: The visitor sees distance become ranking, plays the winner, then moves a
control and sees which reading holds and which does not. The mechanism and the
exact commands below carry the rest.

## Non-goals

No second model. No classifier per label. No claim that a contact sheet is
motion or that audio is unembeddable — the plate reports a measured edge of one
model reading one picture.
